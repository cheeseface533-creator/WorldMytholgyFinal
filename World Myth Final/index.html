The Myth of the Mahaha

The Mahaha From Inuit folklore, is a demon that treks across the arctic landscape. It appears as a skeleton, wearing ratted clothes. Across its face, it has a wide, almost cold grin. It has long nails on its hand, used only for one thing. Hunting. As it treks across the arctic landscape it rings out a deep maniac laughter, luring hunters from tribes out into the harsh cold. When The Mahaha finds its prey, it strikes. Using its long nails it tickles the victim to death. Leaving a permanent cold grin left across their lifeless face. It's said that it is possible to trick the Mahaha. The Mahaha is very arrogant and will play along with the games of the hunters before it kills them, one way to trick it is to ask it to get you one last drink from the cold arctic water. When it bends over to scoop it out, you can shove it in the water hole. Dragging him underneath the water.
